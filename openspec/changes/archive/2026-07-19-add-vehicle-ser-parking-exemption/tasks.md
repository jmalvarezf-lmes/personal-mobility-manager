- [x] 11.4 **Bug 3 — scheduled SER zone ingestion crashed for Madrid with `ForeignKeyViolation` on `fk_vehicle_ser_parking_exemptions_zone_area`** (found live in the running `api` container's logs, once a vehicle had a saved exemption): `PostgresSerZoneRepository.bulk_replace()` used to blanket `DELETE FROM ser_zone_areas WHERE city_code = ...` then re-insert fresh rows on every run; the moment any `vehicle_ser_parking_exemptions` row referenced a still-live `(city_code, zone_number)`, the DELETE violated the composite FK (no `ON DELETE` action) and aborted the whole city's ingestion.
